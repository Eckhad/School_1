ABOUT US

A School That Knows Every Student

Since 1994, we've prepared young people from across Dar es Salaam for higher education and for life — through small class sizes, attentive teachers, and equal emphasis on academics and character.

Our Mission

To deliver an excellent, inclusive education that equips students to think critically, communicate with confidence, and serve their communities with integrity.

We believe every student carries a distinct capability — our work is to find it and grow it.

Why Families Choose Us

A 1:17 teacher-to-student ratio, the national curriculum enriched with English and ICT, and a guidance team that tracks every student's progress.

ACADEMICS

Our Subject Offering

From Form I to Form VI, students study a broad curriculum before choosing their specialised track in their third year.

FORM I–VI

Sciences

Biology, Chemistry, and Physics, with hands-on lab work every week.

FORM I–VI

Mathematics

A carefully sequenced curriculum with an extra support period built in.

FORM I–VI

Languages: Kiswahili & English

Reading and writing fluency in both languages, with an emphasis on clear communication.

FORM III–VI

ICT & Business Studies

Practical computer skills alongside the basics of accounting and entrepreneurship.

FORM I–IV

History & Geography

Tanzanian and world history, paired with hands-on geography fieldwork.

FORM I–VI

Arts & Sport

Music, performing arts, and inter-house football and athletics leagues.
